A traffic circuit in English, out loud.

CALLSIGNF-ABCD
GROUND121.805
STEP–/–

You are F-ABCD, a light aircraft at Isola, a fictional controlled aerodrome. Taxi out, fly a touch and go, then a second circuit to land, and taxi back. The tower talks to you; you answer out loud.

  • Space is your press-to-talk: hold it while you speak, release when you're done. On a phone, hold the round button.
  • Read back what has to be read back, with your callsign at the end. If the tower says negative, read back the corrected part.
  • Say say again (or press the button) to hear the last message again.

    A practice game, not training. It is not an approved training device, not an assessment, and not a preparation for any language proficiency check. The aerodrome, frequencies and traffic are made up. Phraseology differs between countries: your instructor and your national authority's manual are the reference.
